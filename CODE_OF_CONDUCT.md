# Code of conduct

Baton follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository titled "Conduct report", or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.
