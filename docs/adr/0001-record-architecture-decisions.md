# 0001: Keep architecture decision records

Status: accepted

## Context

Baton is open source and will have contributors who were not present for early decisions. The reasons for a decision are usually lost long before the code changes.

## Decision

Record decisions that are costly to reverse in `docs/adr/`, one short file each, with context, the decision, alternatives and consequences. Number them in order. Supersede instead of rewriting.

## Consequences

A small writing cost at decision time. Contributors can read why things are the way they are, and challenge a decision by proposing a new record.
